CRASHER CRUST / HARDCORE PUNK|Montreal-Canada

Synaptic Isolation Syndrome is a raw crasher crust and hardcore punk band based in Montreal. Featuring ex and current members of notable local acts like Warkrusher, Ballast, Mueco, and Dark Circles, they play aggressive, noisy punk heavily influenced by the Japanese D-beat and stenchcore scenes.
